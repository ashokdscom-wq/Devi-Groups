Terms & Conditions of Trade | DEVI GROUPS

1. Commercial Terms & Pricing (Incoterms 2020)

All global exports, quotations, and proforma invoices issued by DEVI GROUPS are based on standard FOB (Indian Ports), CIF, or CFR terms agreed mutually. Prices remain valid for the period specified on official proformas.

2. Quality Assurance & Lab Approvals

Chemicals, Food Colours, Intermediates, and Packaging materials adhere to strict certified specifications (FSSAI, APEDA, EN 71-3). Pre-shipment batch analysis and sample testing approvals are provided before container dispatch.

3. Export Packing & Cargo Integrity

Heavy duty multi-ply corrugated cartons, poly-lined moisture proof HDPE bags, and tamper-evident shrink wrapping are utilized for all containerized shipments to eliminate transit damage.

4. Payment & Documentation

Payments are handled via Letter of Credit (L/C at sight), Bank Wire Transfer (T/T), or advance terms as confirmed in the sales contract. Full shipping documentation including Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, and Invoice/Packing list are promptly couriered.

5. Jurisdiction & Governance

DEVI GROUPS operates as a registered single-proprietor enterprise established in 1989. All legal agreements and commercial contracts are subject to the jurisdiction of Ahmedabad, Gujarat (India).
